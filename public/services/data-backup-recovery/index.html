Data, Backup & Recovery

Automated on-site and cloud backups, disaster recovery plans and database management so your records are never lost.

Data, Backup & Recovery in South Sudan

Hardware failure, theft, fire, power surges or ransomware can wipe out years of records in seconds. Many organizations only discover their backups don't work when it's too late.

ONOX sets up automated, tested backups both on-site and in the cloud, and prepares a recovery plan so you can restore your data and get back to work quickly.

What's Included

  • Backup assessment and strategy
  • Automated on-site backup (NAS and servers)
  • Encrypted cloud backup
  • Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace backup
  • Disaster recovery planning
  • Regular restore testing
  • Database administration and optimization
  • Data recovery from failed drives

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should we back up?

Most organizations need daily automated backups. Critical systems may need more frequent backups. We recommend a schedule based on your needs.

Can you recover data from a broken hard drive?

In many cases, yes. We assess the drive and advise on the best recovery option.

Where is cloud backup data stored?

Data is stored in secure, encrypted international data centres from reputable providers.
